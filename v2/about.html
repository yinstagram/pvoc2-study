Known Limitations — PVOC2 Self-Study Site

Last updated: 2026-05-10 Site status: Staging build, not yet production-approved

This document lists everything that may be incomplete, inferred, or unverified in this site. Read this BEFORE relying on the site to prepare for the actual exam.

1. Site is unofficial

This website is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by: - 香港海事處 (Hong Kong Marine Department) - 高峰進修學院考試中心 (PEAK Examination Centre) - 職業訓練局 (Vocational Training Council)

All content was compiled by an AI assistant (Claude, by Anthropic) from public materials on mardep.gov.hk and peak.edu.hk as of 2026-05-10. Before booking an exam or making any decision, verify current fees, requirements, and procedures on the official sites.

2. Answer key verification status

✓ All 80 mock-exam answer keys (Part A 1–40, Part B 1–40) have been verified verbatim against the official Marine Department PDF answer pages by independent deep-research audit on 2026-05-10.

Note: Caveat: The verification script (verify_answers.py) re-confirms this against live PDFs at deploy time. If the Marine Department updates the mock exams in future, the baked-in answer keys may drift. The script aborts deployment if it detects a mismatch.

3. Explanations are integrated, not official

The explanation_tc field on each question is Claude's integrated explanation drawing on COLREGS rules, the Marine Department PVOC2 Examination Guidebook, and standard maritime references. It is not the official PEAK or Marine Department answer rationale (no such public rationale exists for the mock exams).

Implication: The reasoning may be correct in substance but use phrasing or emphasis that differs from what a PEAK examiner would write. Use these explanations to understand WHY an answer is correct, not as official authority.

4. Six questions have unverified images

Questions A.19, A.20, A.22, A.27, B.1, B.22 reference images embedded in the official PDFs. These images could not be extracted in this build (PDF embedded raster image extraction failed). For each: - The site shows a textual description of what the image most likely depicts (based on the question text + answer + COLREGS reasoning). - The site excludes these 6 questions from the timed mock exam score (so the mock simulator scores out of 74/80, not 80/80). - Each question card includes a direct link to the official PDF so you can view the actual image yourself.

The Q19 distress signal image specifically is most likely COLREG Annex IV §1(g) "square flag + ball", but this is an inference by elimination — not visually confirmed from any independent source. See sources.html → Known Uncertainties.

5. Logistics chapter may be incomplete

The site covers the broad PVOC2 process (PEAK exam → MD687 + MD818 medicals → MD682 application + HK$220 → certificate), but some operational details are summarised from the latest available sources and may have changed: - PEAK fee: HK$580 per paper per the PEAK MD page as of 2026-05-10. However, PEAK's homepage news ticker also references HK$785 effective 13 Dec 2025. The most likely interpretation is HK$785 (Dec 2025 – Mar 2026) → HK$580 (Apr 2026 onwards) but this should be re-checked on the PEAK live page before you book. - Booking lead times: at least 10 working days (post) or 3 working days (online) before exam date. - MD687 validity: 24 months for under-65, 12 months for 65+. - MD818 first effective date: 30 June 2025 (all applicants from this date forward). - Re-sit policy: at least 28 days between attempts; partial pass retained 2 years. - PEAK test centre address: 1/F, VTC Tower, 27 Wood Road, Wanchai.

If any of these are critical to your timing or budget, confirm directly with PEAK (https://www.peak.edu.hk/exam/en/md) or the Marine Department.

6. Some claims softened pending corroboration

  • HKIAAA Area 3 "18-point polygon": The 2022-05-31 amendment to Cap. 313A Fifth Schedule per MDN 105/2022 is confirmed, but the specific "18-point" count was not independently corroborated against a primary source. The site refers instead to "boundary re-described with 13 new boundary light buoys".
  • VTC backup phone: Sources disagree on the secondary number. The PVOC Guidebook (May 2021) lists 2233 7802; the GovHK Safety Tips page lists 2233 7808. Both are shown as alternates; 2233 7801 (primary) and MRCC 2233 7999 are undisputed.

7. Source freshness

The PVOC Guidebook used as a primary reference is the May 2021 edition. Some operational details are now superseded by later Marine Department Notices (MDN): - MDN 23/2018 — VHF sector channels (still current). - MDN 75/2023 — increased Principal Fairway count from 13 to 16, effective 5 May 2023. - MDN 105/2022 — HKIAAA Area 3 + 7 boundary re-description. - MDN 109/2025 — K3 Hebe Haven 5-knot speed limit year-round.

If you spot an apparent conflict between the site and an MDN dated after May 2021, trust the MDN.

9. What this site does NOT cover

  • Practical boat handling — passing PVOC2 is the operator certificate; actual hands-on training (steering, mooring, MOB drill, anchoring practice) is separate. Aberdeen Boat Club, Hong Kong Sailing Federation, Sea Dynamics, A&M Boating and others offer practical courses.
  • Vessel licensing — PVOC2 covers the OPERATOR; the VESSEL needs a separate Class IV operating licence + HK$5M third-party insurance (HK$10M if >12 passengers for hire) under Cap. 548.
  • Sea trial / practical assessment — PVOC2 is a written exam only. There is no on-water assessment for Grade 2.
  • Grade 1 PVOC — this site is for Grade 2 (vessels ≤15 m LOA) only. Grade 1 (no length restriction) is a different exam with different content.
  • Commercial certification — PVOC2 is for pleasure vessels. Hire-and-reward / commercial use requires additional certification under different MD regulations.
